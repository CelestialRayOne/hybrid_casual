# ASSETS.md

Every non-code asset that ships in the game is listed here. Rules live in `CLAUDE.md` (Assets section).

Allowed sources:
- **Code-drawn / synthesized**: no entry needed per sprite. Note any third-party *code* (e.g. ZzFX) in `package.json` / licences, not here.
- **Owner-made (ESR)**: art the owner made (largely AI-generated) for the ESR mod, curated, renamed and compressed. Licence: owner-provided; exact terms to be confirmed by the owner. Not covered by the repo MIT licence.
- **CC0 only** for anything from outside.
