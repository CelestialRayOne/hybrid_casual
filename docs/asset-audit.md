# Asset audit: E:\hybrid_casual\hybrid_casual\assets

Audit date: 2026-11-20. Read-only: nothing in the repo was modified, moved, extracted or deleted. Previews and contact sheets were rendered into the scratchpad only. The .rar archives were listed with `7z l` and not extracted.

Rating key: **GREEN** = plausibly owner-original or generic, OK to ship after processing. **AMBER** = unclear provenance or needs a rename or owner confirmation. **RED** = do not ship.

---

## 0. Headline findings (facts)

1. **The top level of `assets/` has 484 loose files (66 MB) that were not in the briefing.** It holds 424 `.sprite` files, 59 `.png` files and 1 `.jpg`. This is the biggest group after `ascendancies/`.
2. **Much of the art carries embedded AI-provenance (C2PA) metadata.** I found it by scanning the file bytes:
   - 16 files in `ascendancies/` (`portraits/*`, `awakened.png`, `battlemage.png`): **OpenAI / ChatGPT, `trainedAlgorithmicMedia`**.
   - Top-level `ChatGPT Image Jun 18…png`, `ChatGPT Image Jun 23…png` and `cooljewel.png`: the same OpenAI manifest.
   - All 37 `.jfif` files under `specializations/{jeweler,archaeologist,blacksmith}/`: **Google, SynthID, `trainedAlgorithmicMedia`**, which points to Gemini/Imagen. The 1408x768 and 1024x1024 sizes are consistent with that.
   - `specializations/{alchemist,archaeologist,blacksmith,jeweler}.png`, `background.jpg` and `socket.png`: a C2PA manifest from Anthropic/Claude ("Claude provided this file … may have created or modified"). These are composites or edits made with Claude.
   - The other PNGs (mythical-uniques, elixirs, final, most of the top level) have **no provenance metadata**. It was stripped, for example by Photoroom. Their visual style matches the AI-generated sets.

   Judgment: most of this collection is **AI-generated rather than hand-drawn**. "Created by the owner" in practice means "generated by the owner with ChatGPT/Gemini". OpenAI's and Google's terms generally assign outputs to the user, so it is usable. But (a) the copyright protection is weak, and (b) several outputs were clearly prompted to depict Blizzard or other third-party IP (Frostmourne, Lich King, Mephisto, Tal Rasha and others). Those remain RED whoever made them.
3. **`.sprite` files are the Diablo II: Resurrected sprite format.**
   - Magic: `SpA1` ×194, `SPa1` ×56 for non-lowend files.
   - Layout: a 40-byte header, then raw RGBA. I decoded several in memory for preview.
   - Most are conversions of PNGs that are also in the folder. Some exist **only** as `.sprite` (see §3). Those can be decoded losslessly to PNG with a few lines of Python.
4. **`ascendancies/berserker.png` (489 bytes) is not an image.** It is an Azure Blob `AuthenticationFailed` XML error page from a failed download.
5. **The `.rar` archives duplicate files that are already in the folder.**
   - `specializations/jeweler/jeweler.rar` (10.5 MB): the same 15 `.jfif` files.
   - `specializations/blacksmith/blacksmith.rar` (5.7 MB): the same 15 files.
   - `icon pack/Icon 1+2 Pack + Quivers Pack.rar`: holds `Icon Pack #1 (kong).rar`, `Icon Pack #2 (kong).rar` and `Quiver Pack #1 (kong).rar`, dated 2020. That is the third-party pack.
6. **`specializations/` has no `alchemist/` subfolder.** The Alchemist icons exist only inside the strip `specializations/alchemist.png`.
7. **The repo LICENSE is MIT, "Copyright (c) 2026 CelestialRayOne".** If assets are committed next to it, they would appear to be MIT-licensed.

## 3. Usefulness for the gem-dig game (judgment)

- **Gem/block tiles (best fit):**
  - `aurastone_{black,blue,green,orange,red,violet,white,yellow}.sprite` (top level). These are 8 colors in one consistent octagonal cut, which is ideal for color-matched blocks. They exist **only as .sprite**; decode the RGBA after the 40-byte header.
  - Special or rare gems: `celestial_shard`, `mystical_tesseract`, `demonic_voidstone`, `infernal_gemstone`, `intoxicating_crystal`, `electrifying_vortex`, all sprite-only at 98x98.
  - Power or element blocks: `ascendancies/stones/{earth,fire,storm,water,wind}stone.png` and `stoneofcreation.png` (196x196).
  - Avoid `chipped_/flawed_/normal_*` gems because of their D2 shapes and tiers.
- **Relics/collectibles:**
  - `alien_artefact_1-5`, `nokozan_relic`, `eternal_hourglass`, `maras_kaleidoscope`, `the_eye_of_etlich` and `talisman_fukurokuju` (all .sprite).
  - Top-level PNGs: `dwarf_star`, `crescentmoon`, `nexusstar`, `icestormshard`, `salamanderstone`, `secheronyaspark`, `smiffstone`, `bohemianstone`, `idealcraft`, `hgc`, the `hg1-3` hourglass, `ladyeboshi.png` (scarab, renamed), and `book.png`.
  - The 7 `mythical-uniques/the_ties_that_bind_*` rings (renamed).
  - Elixirs could serve as boosters.
- **Perk-path icons (ready-made):**
  - `specializations/{alchemist,archaeologist,blacksmith,jeweler}.png`: 60 icons color-coded per path. Cut them into 132px tiles or use them as an atlas.
  - The jeweler/archaeologist/blacksmith `.jfif` files work as perk detail art at 256 to 512px.
  - `socket.png`, `ringlearned`, `glow` and `shade` (.sprite) work as node frames for learned and locked states.
- **Backgrounds:**
  - Nothing portrait-ready for gameplay. `specializations/background.jpg` (1279x1440, a grey stone panel) could be a menu panel, but it is drab and D2-ish.
  - Gameplay backgrounds (soil or strata layers) will need new art.
- **UI frames:** `socket.png` and the ring sprites, plus the 20 `final/used images` tier medallions as depth or level badges. The D2R UI parts are not useful.
- **Too dark or gritty for the casual portal audience:**
  - All `ascendancies/` portraits, especially `bloodmage` and `lifebreaker`, which show blood.
  - Skull items: `rathma_eternal_skull`, `cursed_donut`, `nobg_tier5_sanctumofthedead`, `the grim reaper's harvester`, `behelit`, `icon of sin`.
  - The `ChatGPT Image Jun 18` skull frame and `nexusmodsimages`.
  - Sensitivity check needed:
    - `archaeologist/the_third_eye_2_3.jfif`: a face covered in a niqab-like cloth with a third eye.
    - `charm.png` and `jeweler/the_perfect_form_5_2.jfif`: Kokopelli, a Native American sacred figure.
- **Size cost:** I test-encoded to WebP at q80 in memory, nothing written:
  - 4 perk strips (60 icons): about 262 KB
  - 6 elemental orbs at 128px: about 21 KB
  - 8 sprite gems at 98px: about 40 KB
  - 17 elixirs: about 47 KB
  - `background.jpg` at 720px wide: about 30 KB
  - one 512px perk illustration: about 21 KB

  A full curated set of roughly 120 icons, about 10 illustrations and a background should land around **0.6 to 1.0 MB**, well inside 8 MB. The 1024px masters (1 to 2 MB each) must never ship raw. Downscale them to 2x their display size and pack the small icons into atlases.

## 5. Repo hygiene (recommendation)

- Keep `assets/` (213 MB of raw sources, including RED material) **out of git**. Move it outside the repo, for example to `E:\hybrid_casual\source-art\`, or add `/assets/` to `.gitignore` now, before any `git add .`. Also ignore `*.sprite`, `*.dc6`, `*.rar`, `*.jfif` masters and `nexusmodsimages/`.
- Commit only curated, processed output, for example `public/art/` with WebP or PNG atlases. Add a `ART_PROVENANCE.csv` listing source file, generator (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude), new name, rating and owner sign-off.
- Add an asset-license note separate from the code's MIT license, because the AI-generated art should not be implicitly MIT-licensed.
- Use Git LFS only if raw masters must be versioned. Even then, never include the RED folders.
- Delete-candidates for the owner to decide, outside the repo: `berserker.png` (broken), the duplicate .rar files, and duplicate PNGs.
- Do a final pre-ship grep of `dist/` for the names in §2 and for `diablo|blizzard|esr|resurrected`.
